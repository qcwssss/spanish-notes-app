# Design Context

**Target audience**:
New visitors/prospective users (authenticated users are redirected away).

**Use cases**:
To introduce VivaNote (a language learning/note-taking app), explain its value, and convert visitors into signing up/logging in.

**Brand personality/tone**:
A blend of clean, lightweight, high-efficiency minimalism (like Notion/Vercel) but softened with warmth and approachability suited for language learning. It should feel professional and mostly minimal, but inviting and gentle rather than cold or starkly technical.
